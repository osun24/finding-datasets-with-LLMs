Title: OC-MS/MS -  HSP60-regulated Mitochondrial Proteostasis and Protein Translation Promote Tumor Growth of Ovarian Cancer
Description: Ovarian cancer (OC) is the most lethal gynecological carcinoma because of the lack of early diagnostic markers and effective drug targets. Discovery of new therapeutic targets in OC to improve the treatment outcome is urgently needed. Patients with recurrent EOC after initial therapy have few treatment options that greatly compromises their quality of life and life expectancy. Discovery of new therapeutic targets in EOC to improve the treatment outcome is urgently needed. Proteomic analysis is one of the approaches to identify therapeutic targets in OC.
