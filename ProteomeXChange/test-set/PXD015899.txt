Title: Time-Dependent Proteome Study: Human ovarian cancer tumor samples (0, 5, 30, 60 min)
Description: The objective of the Time-Dependent Proteome Study, was to evaluate changes in the proteome and phosphoproteome when there is a delay in freezing tissues following sample (tumor) excision. The biospecimens used are from patient-derived ovarian cancer tumors.Ref: Mertins P et al., (2014) Mol Cell Proteomics, Apr 9. E-PublicationRef: Gajadhar, A.S., et al., (2015) Cancer Res.
