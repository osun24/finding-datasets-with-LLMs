**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. The dataset must be about human microarray expression profiling. 
2. The dataset must involve samples from CNS regions relevant to (AD, LBD, or ALS-FTD). 

**Exclusion criteria:** 
1. Not human brain tissue.
2. Not pertaining to disease in question (i.e., AD, LBD, or ALS-FTD).
3. Patient-derived in vitro cell lines or disease models.
4. Study designs other than case/control.
5. Brain region not significantly affected by neurodegeneration (e.g., cerebellum in AD)
6. Exclude intermediate phenotypes (e.g., Braak III/IV in AD)
7. Exclude familial AD and LBD datasets
8. Incompatible technologies to microarray expression profiling (e.g., RNA-seq, proteomics, etc.)

Removed:
(1) original datasets from inclusion criteria — duplicates and re-analyses were excluded already in retrieval
